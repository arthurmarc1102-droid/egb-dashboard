 
Ministero dell’Economia e delle Finanze 
PRESS RELEASE 
Placement details of the double tap of the 7-year and 30-year BTP 
 
The Ministry of Economy and Finance announces the placement details of the syndicated issuance 
double tap of the 7-year BTP, due 15th of June 2033, and the 30-year BTP, due 1st of October 2055. 
The total amount issued has been 18 billion Euros (13 billion Euros for the 7-year and 5 billion 
Euros for the 30-year BTP), while the overall demand has reached 238 billion Euros, of which 126 
billion Euros for the 7-year BTP and 112 billion Euros for the 30-year BTP. Moreover, around 300 
investors have taken part in the 7-year BTP issuance and over 330 in the 30-year one.

Both placements saw an extremely diversified participation (more than 35 countries for both 
issuances), with a large interest by foreign investors who subscribed to 83.7% and 89% of the 7-
year and 30-year BTP issuances, respectively.

The bonds have been placed through a syndicate structured with six lead managers, Banco 
Santander SA, BNP Paribas, BofA Securities Europe S.A., Goldman Sachs Bank Europe SE, J.P. 
Morgan SE and Morgan Stanley Europe SE, with the rest of Specialists in Italian Government 
bonds participating as co-lead managers.
